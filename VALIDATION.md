## Moving terrain window — 11 October 2026

Replaced the fixed nine-region download/scene handover with a camera-centred streaming window. Entry prepares an 8 km radius, then expands in the background to a zoom/tilt-dependent 16–24 km horizon plus a 3 km retention margin. Movement of 2 km schedules the next window.
